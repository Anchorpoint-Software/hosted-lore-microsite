GitHub-like hosted Lore repositories are coming

        

          
You probably heard about Lore, the new version control system from Epic Games.

          

          
If you don’t care about managing servers and all the hassles with configuring AWS, we do that for you at a fair price.

        

      


      

        
Why not today?

        

          
Lore is still pre v1.0. We are building the infrastructure around it but it’s just too early to say that it’s “production ready”.

          

          
However if you want to test it, you can access the beta version.

        

      

      Access the beta

      

        
Who we are

        

          
We are Anchorpoint, and have built version control tooling around Git since 2020.

          

          
We serve companies such as Innogames, Service Plan Group or Yostar.

        

        
Our solution is also part of the official Unreal Engine documentation.

      


      

        
        
        
        
        
      


      

        
Is Lore the right thing for you?

        

          
It is the right thing when you work on large binary-heavy projects, because it’s fast and has features to handle large projects such as chunking, repository layers and a virtual file system.

          

          
-- How Lore compares to Git and Perforce --

          

            
Lore: 6m

            
Perforce: 1h 23m

            
Git LFS: 1h 6m

          

          
We have pushed 392,559 files with a total size of 63.5 GB on a 790 Mbps upload speed.

          

        

        
It is not the right thing for you when your projects are light, such as mobile development. Git is more than enough here. Workflow wise when it comes to branching or pull requests it’s the same as Git.

      


      

        
Want to play around with Lore today?

        

          
We have a slick desktop GUI for Lore that you can download for free. Use it on your Lore repositories.

          
You can also sign up to our mailing list or reach out to our CEO directly for a demo.
